# Trade Terms Negotiator

## Purpose

Prepare practical B2B trade terms for distributors, retailers, importers, and pharmacy channels.

## When to Use

Use this skill when discussing price, MOQ, payment timing, territory, exclusivity, marketing support, delivery terms, and performance conditions.

## Inputs

Useful inputs: buyer type, product, price list, MOQ, margin, payment terms, territory, exclusivity request, marketing budget, delivery terms, and target outcome.

## Workflow

1. Define business goal and minimum acceptable terms.
2. Separate fixed terms from flexible terms.
3. Identify risks and trade-offs.
4. Prepare counter options.
5. Recommend next position and approval notes.

## Output

Always provide term summary, fixed terms, flexible terms, risk notes, counter options, and next steps.

## Common Mistakes

- Giving exclusivity without targets.
- No minimum acceptable terms.
- Ignoring margin.
- No written summary.

## Terms on the Phone (Das Experten Commerce, 2026-10-10)

- Price, deferral, consignment, credit, exclusivity, territory and minimum volume are never agreed on a call. Say who decides and return with a written answer by a named date.
- Objection algorithm: listen → join the concern (never confirm the claim) → one question → short argument from a true fact → end with a question.
- No discount as a closing lever, no price-matching. Urgency from real stock and real dates: say stock is running low only when it is (checked the same day); "this item often sells out because demand is high" when that is the company's record; sailing date, open territory, a promo date.
- Recognise the same persuasion levers when the buyer uses them ("everyone gives us 90 days", "decide by Friday") — note them, do not react.
