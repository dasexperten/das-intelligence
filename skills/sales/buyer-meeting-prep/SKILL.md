# Buyer Meeting Prep

## Purpose

Prepare for buyer, distributor, importer, or retail meetings with a clear agenda, facts, questions, proof, and next step.

## When to Use

Use this skill before B2B calls, trade fair meetings, distributor meetings, retail buyer meetings, and follow-up negotiations.

## Inputs

Useful inputs: company, buyer role, country, product, meeting goal, previous messages, open questions, pricing notes, proof points, and concerns.

## Workflow

1. Define meeting goal and decision maker.
2. Summarize known context.
3. Prepare talking points and proof.
4. Prepare questions to ask.
5. Define likely outcomes and next steps.

## Output

Always provide meeting brief, agenda, talking points, questions, risks, and next step.

## Common Mistakes

- No meeting goal.
- No questions prepared.
- Too much product detail.
- No follow-up action.

## Before-Dial Card for Calls (Das Experten Commerce, 2026-10-10)

- Four lines about the person, not the company: what they fear losing · how they want to look inside their company · what they say out loud · what blocks the yes.
- One trigger: why this company this month. No trigger — admit the call is cold and ask for 30 seconds.
- Set the agenda in the first minute and get a "yes"; ask 3–4 needs questions and confirm the summary before offering anything.
- Every call ends in one of three results: agreement, a dated callback we make, or a clear refusal. Full call craft: `sales-caller` (das-architektura).
